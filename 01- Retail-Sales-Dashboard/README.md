# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, regional performance, category performance, monthly sales trends, and customer contribution.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using PivotTables, Pivot Charts, slicers, and KPI metrics to provide a clear and interactive view of business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to transform retail sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify key business trends, high-performing categories, regional performance, sales trends, and high-value customers.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

The analysis focused on:

- Sales and profit performance by category
- Sales distribution by region
- Monthly sales trends
- Top 10 customers by sales
- Interactive filtering by category, region, and state

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **West** leads regional sales, contributing **32%** of total sales.
- **Technology** generates the highest category sales at **$836,154** and the highest category profit at **$145,455**.
- **Sean Miller** is the top customer among the Top 10, with **$25,043** in sales.
- Sales peaked in **November**, indicating strong late-year demand.

## 💡 Recommendation

Focus promotional efforts on the **West region** and prioritize the **Technology category**. Increase marketing activity during the stronger Q4 sales period to capitalize on seasonal demand and drive further revenue growth.
